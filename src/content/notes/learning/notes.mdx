## Start with a question

Before copying a whole page of information, ask: **What question is this note answering?**

A note is useful when your future self can still understand it. Length is secondary.

## A small template

1. State the question in one sentence.
2. Explain the idea in your own words.
3. Add an example you can run or verify.
4. Record what you are still uncertain about.
5. Link related notes and references.

## Revisit instead of finishing everything at once

Treat notes like a garden: plant something small, then add, trim, and connect it as you learn.

> An unfinished question can be a starting point for your next learning session.
